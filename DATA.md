# Bundled Market Data

MockMarket ships generated OHLCV data for educational simulations. The files are not a
record of actual market history and must not be used to verify performance or make
investment decisions.

## Provenance

`scripts/generate_data.js` generates every file in `public/data/stocks`,
`public/data/etfs`, and `public/data/crypto`; it does not contact a market-data vendor or
API. The ticker universe and static company metadata come from `src/model/tickers.ts` and
`public/data/tickers.json`.

Some widely followed symbols use manually entered, approximate price anchors to shape
their synthetic paths. Most symbols use generic seeded anchors. The anchors are unsourced,
are not guaranteed to match an official close, and are not themselves copied directly into
the output. Prices between anchors, OHLC wicks, and all volume values are generated by a
deterministic pseudo-random process.

## Coverage And Calendar

- Date range: `2015-01-01` through `2024-12-31` for crypto and `2015-01-02` through
  `2024-12-31` for equities and ETFs.
- Dates are UTC calendar labels, not exchange timestamps.
- Equity and ETF dates use the NYSE/Nasdaq full-day calendar supplied by
  `us-equity-market-calendar`, plus an explicit exceptional closure for December 5, 2018.
- Early-close sessions remain represented as ordinary daily candles; session duration is
  not modeled.
- Crypto uses every calendar day, including weekends and equity holidays.
- The calendar and exceptional closures must be reviewed before extending the date range.

## Schema And Method

Each JSON file is an ascending array of `{ time, open, high, low, close, volume }` daily
candles. A ticker-specific seed makes generation reproducible while ticker order and the
calendar remain unchanged. The generator interpolates between reference anchors, applies a
bounded random move and drift toward the current target, creates synthetic high/low wicks,
and derives synthetic volume from a base level and daily price movement.

Run `node scripts/generate_data.js` to regenerate all datasets. Regeneration intentionally
overwrites the committed files.

## Limitations

- No intraday trades, quotes, bid/ask spreads, auctions, halts, or early-close durations.
- No consistent split or dividend adjustment policy. Some anchors are approximately
  split-adjusted, but corporate actions are not processed.
- Dividends are not paid into simulations, so results are price-path returns rather than
  total returns.
- No IPO dates, fund inception dates, delistings, ticker changes, constituent changes, or
  survivorship-bias correction. Every symbol receives the common date range.
- Static market cap, valuation, sector, and yield metadata are unsourced and are not
  point-in-time series.
- Historical scenarios and catalyst text are editorial educational content; they do not
  make the generated candles factual records of those events.

The intended uses are UI demonstrations, deterministic tests, and strategy education.
The data is unsuitable for historical research, model validation, financial advice, or
live trading decisions.
